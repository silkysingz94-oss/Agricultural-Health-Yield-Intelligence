Methodology and Analytical Workflow

Proposed integration of field data, Earth observation and climate information for agricultural monitoring.

01 • Field Data

Crop type, planting date, harvested area, crop-cut measurements, observed crop condition and GPS locations.

02 • Satellite Observations

Sentinel-2 vegetation indices including NDVI, EVI and NDMI; Sentinel-1 radar information for moisture and crop structure screening.

03 • Climate Variables

Rainfall, temperature, humidity and soil moisture indicators from verified station or gridded climate sources.

04 • Agricultural Indicators

Vegetation condition, rainfall anomaly, moisture status and relative crop-health screening.

05 • Yield Modelling

Train and validate crop-specific models using measured crop-cut yield observations and independent validation samples.

06 • Statistical Reporting

Summarise results by region, district, crop type and relevant administrative or survey geography.

Statistical Interpretation and Data Quality

Yield: The average yield shown is calculated from the sample records currently displayed. It is not a regional or national crop-yield estimate.

NDVI: NDVI is a vegetation greenness indicator. It should not be interpreted as a direct measurement of crop yield.

Crop condition: Good, Moderate and Poor are illustrative field-assessment categories.

Weather: The rainfall and climate charts use demonstration observations, not official GMet station data.

Model validation: A supervised yield model should be evaluated using independent validation data and appropriate metrics such as RMSE, MAE and R².
